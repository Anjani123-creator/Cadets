What Are Canadian Cadets?

The Canadian Cadet Program is a national youth program that helps young people develop leadership, citizenship, teamwork, and physical fitness.

There are three different elements: Sea, Army, and Air Cadets. Each offers unique activities and opportunities.

Cadets learn to take responsibility, support their teams, challenge themselves, and contribute to their communities.
