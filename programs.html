The Three Cadet Programs

Three elements. Different experiences. A variety.

Home Programs Activities How to Join

01 — Royal Canadian Sea Cadets

Sea Cadets focuses on maritime activities and skills connected to life on the water. Cadets can learn about navigation, sailing, and seamanship.

Activities may include:

  • Sailing and seamanship
  • Navigation and maritime knowledge
  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Community service and ceremonies

02 — Royal Canadian Army Cadets

Army Cadets emphasizes outdoor adventure, teamwork, leadership, and practical field skills. Cadets can build confidence while learning to navigate and work together.

Activities may include:

  • Map reading and orienteering
  • Camping and hiking
  • Outdoor expeditions
  • Leadership and community involvement

03 — Royal Canadian Air Cadets

Air Cadets introduces young people to aviation and aerospace. Depending on eligibility and available opportunities, cadets may participate in flying-related experiences.

Activities may include:

  • Aviation and aerospace studies
  • Air navigation and meteorology
  • Gliding and flying opportunities
  • Leadership, fitness, and drill
