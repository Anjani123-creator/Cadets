Who Can Join?

The Canadian Cadet Program is open to eligible young people who meet the enrolment requirements.

Before You Register

  • Review the official eligibility requirements.
  • Explore Sea, Army, and Air Cadets.
  • Ask a parent or guardian to help with registration.
  • Find a local cadet corps or squadron.

Participating in the Cadet Program does not automatically make someone a member of the Canadian Armed Forces or require a commitment to a military career.
