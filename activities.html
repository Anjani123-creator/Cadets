What Do Cadets Do?

Cadet activities vary between corps and squadrons. Training opportunities depend on the unit, location, experience, and available courses.

Leadership

Practise decision-making, take responsibility, and learn how to support a team.

Drill and Ceremonies

Develop attention to detail, coordination, confidence, and respect for traditions.

Outdoor Experiences

Explore navigation, teamwork, and outdoor learning through supervised activities.

Community Service

Contribute to local events and learn about citizenship and community involvement.

Summer Training

Eligible cadets may have opportunities to attend training courses during the summer.

Friendship and Teamwork

Meet people, work toward shared goals, and build communication skills.

Skills That Last

Leadership, communication, self-discipline, and teamwork are useful at school, at work, and in everyday life.
